## Practise the verses that matter to you

Choose a specific verse or passage and test yourself on its Hebrew or Greek words. Whether you are preparing for a Bible study, working through a passage in class, or returning to a favourite chapter, your flashcards can follow the text you are studying.

This makes vocabulary practice directly relevant to your reading. Instead of working through an unrelated word list, you build familiarity with the words you will meet in your chosen verses.

## Learn the word as it appears in the text

The flashcards test you on **the grammatical form a word takes in the passage**, rather than only its dictionary or base form. Hebrew and Greek words change form according to how they are used, so recognising a dictionary entry is only part of learning to read them.

Practising the actual forms helps you connect what you see in the biblical text with what it means. The aim is to recognise and understand words more naturally, with less need to mentally work back to the base form each time. This complements your study of grammar and helps you put that learning into practice.

## Your passage. Your pace.

- **Choose your passage:** focus on the verses you want to learn.
- **Build recognition:** practise Hebrew and Greek word forms in their biblical context.
- **Test yourself:** use flashcards and timed quizzes to check your understanding.
- **Keep studying offline:** download the app and take your flashcards with you.

Learning the biblical languages can deepen our engagement with God’s word. These flashcards are part of [Exploring Theology’s BibleWiki](https://exploringtheology.com/bible/doku.php?id=start), created to help students become more familiar with the language of Scripture.
